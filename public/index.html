Many shops. One haat.

Like a village haat where every seller has their own stall, Haat lets independent businesses open their own online store on one shared platform. Each store keeps its own products, stock and orders, and no store can see another's.

Three people use Haat

Everyone shares the same application. What each of them can see and change is decided on the server, not by which buttons are shown.

The administrator

Adds businesses to the platform and can switch a business off. Sees totals, never runs anyone's shop.

The store owner

Sets up one store, adds products, publishes them, keeps stock up to date and moves orders from pending to delivered.

The shopper

Browses a single store, fills a cart from that store only, and checks out. Stock and totals are checked on the server.
